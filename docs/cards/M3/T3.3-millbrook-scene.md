# T3.3 The new Millbrook scene (W, worktree `w`, ≈ 5 AD)

**Inputs:** `WorldData\millbrook\v1` (T3.2), `TownData` buildings, the M1/M2 terrain renderer and chunker, the eight
tree species and the grass tufts (`art\`), R's road chunks (R3, when they land), S's site meshes (S4), A's buildings
(T3.4, when they land), ADR-006 §5 W6-W7.

**Steps**
1. `Scenes\Millbrook.unity` (new): the 1,024 terrain chunks with LODs, the river water (two reaches and the spill at the
   dam), sky and sun from M1. Road and site meshes come in from their bakes; until R3 lands, R0o's plain ribbons.
2. **Grey-box buildings:** every footprint extruded to its storeys (tagged, else `buildingProfile.json` by type), a flat
   or simple gable roof by type, a muted colour by type, a box collider set per building. A's models replace them per
   type as they land, through one `BuildingSet` keyed by OSM id.
3. **First nature:** trees from the forest and wood areas and the hollows' slopes (our eight species, density by
   land use), hedgerows along field edges, grass tufts on meadow and verges; nothing on roads, lots, water or plates.
4. **Places (provisional, W7 in M9 is lane G's):** `world.json` `pois` for the garage (vehicle spawn and the four
   bays), mill, fuel, boat landing, farm, and the added quarry, depot and marsh at the scout's spots, each with a pad
   from S's site plan where one exists.
5. Minimap and big map: `map.png` rendered from the bake (roads, water, buildings, contours), replacing v1's.
6. Vehicles spawn at the new garage; the player drives the whole 2 km square.

**Done when:** autotest `m3.shots.millbrook` (12 fixed cameras: the village street, the mill and dam, the bridge, the
landing, the fuel station, the quarry hollow, the marsh, an overview; noon and dusk); perf `m3.perf.flythrough-high`
re-measured on the new world (report, budget set at M5); `Look.NoErrorShaders` on the new scene.
