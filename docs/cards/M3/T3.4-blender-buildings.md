# T3.4 Blender building generator, phase 1 (A, worktree `a`, ≈ 8 AD)

**Inputs:** ADR-006 §5 W5, research 07 (art pipeline), the site research's lane A contract (`docs\design\site-layouts.md`
§7a, `DoorSpec`) and `Game\Assets\Content\sites\buildingProfile.json` (footprints, storeys and roof shapes per OSM type,
with the U28 roof data), `TownData\stockport-oh\v1\buildings.json`, the A1 FBX pipeline from M2 (postprocessor, material
remap, LOD groups, `UCX_` colliders), the user's Synty City buildings in the client project (look only).

**The Synty rule (AGENTS.md rule 3):** you may look at and measure the Synty City buildings in Unity (palette,
proportions, trim widths, window sizes, texture style). Our generator and every model it makes are our own: never
start from, trace, edit or convert a Synty mesh, never upload Synty files or renders to an AI tool, never commit them.

**Steps**
1. **Style guide** (`art\buildings\style-guide.md` + a palette texture of our own): measure in Unity what makes the
   Synty City buildings read as one family (flat colour atlas, bevel and trim sizes, window and door proportions,
   storey heights, roof overhangs, saturation and value ranges) and write the numbers down; draw our palette atlas to
   match. A one-page contact sheet of our test blocks beside the Synty ones, kept local (never committed or uploaded).
2. **Generator** (`art\buildings\gen\`, Blender 4.x Python, `blender -b` from `tools\ci\`): reads one building
   instance (footprint polygon, storeys, roof shape and pitch, type, style seed, `DoorSpec`s) and writes an FBX with
   LOD0/LOD1/LOD2, `UCX_` colliders, a window-pane naming scheme for night lighting (M5), on our atlas.
3. **The first five types** for Stockport's mix: the frame **house** (gable, porch, 1-2 storeys), the **brick
   commercial block** (shared side walls, shopfront, parapet), the **barn and machine shed**, the **warehouse or
   shed** (docks where S3's `DoorSpec`s put them), and the **mill** (multi-storey, by the dam). Every other type stays a
   grey box (T3.3) until phase 2 (M4).
4. Batch: all Stockport buildings of those types, deterministic per OSM id (`hash(TOWN_ID, id)`), imported into
   `Game\Assets\Art\Buildings\Millbrook\` through the A1 pipeline, mapped into T3.3's `BuildingSet`.

**Done when:** EditMode `Art.BuildingsBudget` (LOD0 ≤ 6k triangles for a house, ≤ 15k for a block or the mill; ≤ 3
materials; colliders present; panes named), `Art.BuildingsDeterministic` (generate twice → identical FBX hashes), and
`Art.DoorsMatchSites` (every `DoorSpec` from S3 has an opening within 0.1 m); autotest `m3.shots.sites` shows them;
your sign-off sheet: our five types beside the style guide's numbers.
