# T3.2 Terrain from the DEM, the river, land surfaces, plates, the WorldData export (W, worktree `w`, ≈ 6 AD)

**Inputs:** `TownData\stockport-oh\v1` (T3.0: `dem\dem_1m.f32`, `water.json`, `landuse.json`), ADR-006 §3 and §5
(stages W1, W6 surfaces, W8), ADR-007 (`Plate`, `IPlateSink`, the plate order), the CanonData v1 layer formats.

**Steps**
1. **W1 terrain:** resample the 1 m DEM to the 0.5 m grid (bicubic, no new detail invented beyond a seeded 2-5 cm
   micro-noise outside roads and lots, kept below the lidar's own noise); `base` = the resampled DEM; `heights` = base
   after plates. Bridges, the dam and buildings come out of a bare-earth DEM already; check the bridge approaches and
   the lock walls by eye against the scout's map.
2. **The river:** a water surface per reach from `water.json` (upstream and downstream of the dam at their own levels;
   the drop at the dam), the riverbed carved 1.5-3 m below the surface where the lidar shows water returns, banks
   smoothed over 3 m. The lock is a walled channel; the dam a weir the water spills over (visual only in M3).
3. **Land surfaces:** `surface`, `mud` and `tint` from `landuse.json` and slope: farmland (dirt rows), meadow, forest
   floor, gravel, the marsh at the creek mouth (bog mud; added per the scout's site list), riverbank mud, rock on
   slopes over 60 %.
4. **Plates API** (`IPlateSink`): accept plates from R (roads), S (sites) and building pads, apply them in ADR-007's
   fixed order with blend bands, and hash the result. Same plates in → same bytes out, Burst on and off.
5. **W8 export:** `WorldData\millbrook\v1\` in the CanonData v1 layer formats at the 4,097² layout, with
   `manifest.json` (hashes, layout, the `TownData` hash it came from, the bake commit, the plate list hash).

**Done when:** EditMode `World.BakeDeterministic` (bake twice, also in a fresh worktree → identical hashes),
`World.ManifestHashes`, `World.RiverLevels` (water surface flat per reach within 2 cm; nothing dry below it inside the
river polygon), `World.DemFidelity` (outside roads, lots and the river, `heights` within 0.10 m of the lidar at 1,000
seeded points).
