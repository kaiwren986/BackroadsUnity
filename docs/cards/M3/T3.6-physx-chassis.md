   boxes, then A's `UCX_`), road furniture and kerbs (R2/R3), site props and islands (S4), trees (T3.3). This replaces
   the JS 2D statics and vehicle-vehicle SAT in both worlds.

**Done when:** PlayMode `Vehicle.BuildingImpact` (the truck driven into a building at 10 and 20 m/s stops at the wall
with no tunnelling) passes in both worlds; every M2 vehicle test still passes on the test world.
