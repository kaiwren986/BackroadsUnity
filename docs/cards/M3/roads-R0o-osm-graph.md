# roads-R0o: the OSM road graph on the real ground (R, worktree `r`, ≈ 3 AD) — draft

**Inputs:** `TownData/<TOWN_ID>/v1/roads.json` (sites-S0), lane W's DEM heightfield for the town (3DEP, `DEM_RES`), the R0 code on `main` (`RoadGraphInput`, `RoadGraphBuilder`, `RoadNetworkAsset`, the debug views), research 05 §5.1-§5.2.

**Steps**
1. `OsmRoadImporter` fills `RoadGraphInput` from OSM ways instead of `world.json`: one polyline per way, resampled to ≤ 2 m, split at shared OSM nodes. Class from `highway`: motorway/trunk/primary/secondary/tertiary (+ `_link`) → `Highway`, which the town zone turns into `MainStreet` (05 §5.1 step 3); residential/unclassified/living_street → `Street`; service → `Driveway`/`Yard` by `service=*` (alley, parking_aisle, driveway, drive-through); track and `surface=unpaved|gravel|dirt|ground` → `Dirt`. Skip footway, path, cycleway, steps, construction, proposed, private service roads inside mapped parking lots (they belong to sites).
2. Cross section from tags, in this order: `width` → `lanes` × 3.3 m (+ 2.4 m per side with `parking:*=lane|parallel` or the newer `parking:both=*`) → class defaults (Highway 7.2 m, MainStreet 12-14 m kerb to kerb when parking is tagged or the street is in `DOWNTOWN_ZONE`, Street 9.0 m, rural Street 6.2 m, Dirt 5.4 m, Yard 6.0 m). `oneway`, `bridge`/`layer`, `tunnel` and `junction=roundabout` are kept on the edge; `sidewalk=*` feeds R2.
3. Heights from the DEM: sample the centreline every 2 m, smooth (05's `makeRoad` recipe: box r 12 m, 4 passes), limit grades (paved 11 %, dirt 15 %, with the real road's own grade as the floor), bridge decks as straight lines between the abutments. Junction nodes get one height (the major road's) before smoothing so arms meet flush.
4. Reuse R0's cleanup unchanged where it is generic: T-splits, 1 m clustering, zone split, joins and L-bends, dead ends, complex pairs (< 30 m) flagged, **major road by class first**. Remove what was Millbrook-specific (legacy node ids, the square, the canon hash checks) behind the old importer, which stays for the CanonData test world.
5. Controls from OSM nodes on the graph: `highway=stop` (with `direction` or `stop=all`), `highway=give_way`, `highway=traffic_signals`, `crossing=*`; store them on the approach arm; 05 §6.2's class rules fill only what the tags leave open.
6. `RoadNetworkAsset` format version 2 (adds tag-derived cross sections, controls with their source, bridge spans); the debug overlay and plan map work on the new town.
7. **Play-area edge** (Stockport: the 2 km × 2 km `PLAY_AREA`): build the graph on the extract (`PLAY_AREA` + 250 m) so junctions just outside are known, then clip to `PLAY_AREA`; every road that crosses the edge ends by `EDGE_RULE` (50 m inside the edge, closed off by the terrain) and is listed in the report.

**Done when:** EditMode tests on `TownData` fixtures (a 600 × 600 m clip of two measured towns, committed under `Tests/EditMode/Roads/Fixtures/` with the ODbL credit): every OSM way of a drivable class is in the graph or listed as skipped with a reason; node kinds and arm counts match a hand-checked table for the fixtures; the graph is connected; every tagged control lands on the right approach; heights are within 0.05 m of the smoothed DEM profile and no paved grade exceeds 11 % except where the real road is steeper (reported); the bake is deterministic (two bakes, same hash).

**Gate:** none alone (feeds R1-R4 and sites-S1). Report: junction count by kind, controls by source (tag vs rule), complex pairs needing a decision, steepest approaches.
