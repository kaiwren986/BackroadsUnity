# T2.4 Engine sound in vehicles (V, worktree `vs`, ≈ 3 AD)

**Inputs:** ESP v1 (M1, ADR-002 frozen), `EngineSimEngine`, `EngineCatalog`, PLAN §3.5 (the M2 rpm map) and §3.6, M1 report "Carried to M2", `Backroads.Core.IVehicleEngine` / `VehicleEngines` / `EngineState`.

**Steps**
1. Implement `IVehicleEngine` on top of `EngineSimEngine` in FOLLOW_RPM: each fixed step, `rpm_es = idle_es + (rpm_js − idle_js)·(0.97·redline_es − idle_es)/(redline_js − idle_js)` from `EngineState`, with the real pedal (throttle) and ignition/starter; `DisplayRpm` = the mapped rpm; `AttachTo(exhaust)` parents the AudioSource. Register the factory with `VehicleEngines.Register` at start-up in client builds.
2. **The truck's gas V8 (U6):** `v8_bigblock_a`, a fictional-id big-block V8, from the piranha script or a C++ port; roster entries for `v8_cross_a`, `f4_boxer_a` and `i4_vvt_a` (or `i4_synth`) for the pickup, rally and hatch.
3. **Release soak underrun (M1 carry):** find and fix the rare ~30 ms underrun in the 10-minute release soak (candidates: MMCSS "Pro Audio" for the worker, the fill target, logging which step stalled). It must be 0 in 3 consecutive soaks.
4. `-br-silent` must not silence `AudioTap` (mute the output another way, or tap before the master).
5. `build-native.ps1 -Heavy` leaves an identical installed DLL alone (no rewrite when the sources are unchanged).
6. A stub-vehicle PlayMode test: `EngineSim.FollowInVehicle` drives `IVehicleEngine` with a scripted `EngineState` ramp and checks engine-sim's rpm tracks the mapped rpm < 40 rpm RMS; per roster engine, real-time factor ≥ 2.0 at High unpinned and ≥ 1.5 on the proxy.

**Done when:** the above tests pass; three release soaks in a row with 0 underruns; the truck engine sounds like a big-block V8 on the Engine Bench.

**Gate:** M2 lane (`EngineSim.FollowInVehicle`, RTF); M2 headline `m2.drive.route9` with T2.1-T2.2 and T2.7 (written by the integrator in T2.10).
