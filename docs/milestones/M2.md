## In plain words

- **Play it:** double-click `builds\M2\Play.bat`. You start in the truck at the Millbrook garage. Drive with W A S D
  (or the arrows), Space is the handbrake, C switches the chase and hood cameras, L lights, H horn, G winch. Drive up
  beside another vehicle at the garage and press E to switch into it.
- **Where to go:** Route 9 to the fuel station, the dirt road to the farm, and into the bog: it ruts and grabs like
  the browser game.
- **The sound:** the truck has a real big-block gas V8 (engine-sim, U6) that follows your foot. Esc, then Engine Bench,
  plays it on its own with a dyno sweep.
- **Menus:** Esc pauses and has Settings (graphics, sound, camera; remembered between runs) and Controls; F1 shows
  the controls.
- **F10:** five questions. The ones that matter most: does it handle like the browser game, and does the sound follow
  your foot (U5a)?

This is the last milestone on the old Millbrook: from M3 the game moves to the new town (Stockport, Ohio, renamed;
U25-U27). The old town stays as the hidden test world that keeps the vehicles checked against the browser game.
